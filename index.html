Questions Tradies Ask

Short answers before you start.

Built for people who want the facts, not a sales ambush.

Does Industry Forms work with Xero?
Yes. Connect your Xero organisation once and your invoices go across automatically — no re-keying, and your accountant sees the same numbers you do.
Does it work without internet?
Yes. Notes, photos, signatures, forms, and time tracking all work offline. Everything queues on the device and syncs automatically the moment you're back in range — nothing is lost.
What happens when my 28-day trial ends?
You'll be prompted to subscribe. Your data is never deleted, but the app locks until you choose a plan — there's no automatic charge and no surprise renewal, you're only ever charged when you actively subscribe.
How long does setup take?
You can be running your first job in about ten minutes, or Josh and Edin will set it up with you personally.
Do I need a credit card?
No. The 28-day trial starts with just an email address.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, in one click from your account settings, and you can export everything first.
Can I export my data?
Yes. Export your jobs, customers, and invoices to CSV any time, including if you leave.
Is there an iPhone app?
Not yet — the iPhone app is in development. The web app works from any phone's browser today, including iPhone.
What is Industry Forms?
Industry Forms is job management software for NZ tradies. It brings quotes, jobs, bookings, forms, schedules, timesheets, invoices, payments, reminders, messaging, and reporting into one workflow.
Can customers accept quotes and pay online?
Yes. Customers can accept quotes, pay deposits, and pay invoices online through secure links without creating an account.
What makes it different from generic trade software?
Industry Forms is built around practical NZ trade workflows: multiple visits per job, offline field capture, real inventory, Xero and Google Calendar sync, Stripe payments, AI quote drafting from your price list, and transparent pricing.
