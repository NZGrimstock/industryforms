Before you ask

The questions we actually get, answered without the sales gloss.

Do I need every feature turned on to start?

No. Most people start with customers, quotes, jobs and invoices and leave the rest switched off until they need it. Nothing has to be configured before you can send your first quote.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The mobile app is offline-first by design. Notes, photos, time, signatures and travel are captured on the device and sync themselves when signal comes back. You do not have to remember to do anything.

Which mobile apps can I get?

The Android app is live on Google Play. The iOS app is still in development and is not on the App Store yet. In the meantime the full web app runs in any phone browser, including on an iPhone.

Will it work with my accountant?

If they use Xero, yes. Connect your organisation once and invoices flow across automatically. If they use something else, you can export everything at any time.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export it at any time, including on the way out. No export fee, no contract, and cancelling takes one click rather than a phone call.

Is this suited to a one-person business?

Yes. Solo exists for exactly that, and the free plan covers a very small operation indefinitely. You are not paying for seats you do not use.

28 days, everything unlocked, no credit card. If it does not fit how you work, walk away and take your data with you.
